## Real Aircraft Background & The Modernized Tu-160M

The **Tupolev Tu-160** (NATO reporting name: *Blackjack*, known to Russian aircrews as the *White Swan* or *Bely Lebed*) remains the largest and heaviest supersonic military aircraft, as well as the fastest operational heavy bomber ever constructed.

Airframe **RF-94105 (Bort Red 06)**, officially christened ***Ilya Muromets*** after the legendary Kievan Rus hero and the pioneering WWI Sikorsky heavy bomber, holds an exceptional place in contemporary aviation history. It served as the lead prototype airframe for the comprehensive **Tu-160M modernization program**, undergoing structural retrofits, next-generation digital glass cockpits, upgraded NVG compatibility, and upgraded Kuznetsov NK-32-02 powerplants. 

In February 2024, RF-94105 drew global media attention when Russian President Vladimir Putin personally took the co-pilot's seat during a high-profile 30-minute demonstration flight from Kazan Aviation Plant, cementing this specific airframe as a cornerstone of modern strategic deterrence.
