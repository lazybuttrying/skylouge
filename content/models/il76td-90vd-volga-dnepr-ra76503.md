## Real Aircraft & The PS-90A Modernization

The **Ilyushin Il-76** is one of the world's most versatile strategic airlifters, having served over 115 air forces and cargo airlines globally. However, as international environmental and noise regulations tightened in the early 2000s, standard D-30KP-powered Il-76 models were banned from European and North American airfields due to ICAO Stage 3 noise infringements.

To maintain its global heavy outsized charter leadership, **Volga-Dnepr Airlines** partnered with Ilyushin and the Tashkent Aviation Plant (TAPO) to develop the **Il-76TD-90VD**. Powered by state-of-the-art **Aviadvigatel PS-90A-76** high-bypass turbofans with digital FADEC, this variant achieved full compliance with ICAO Chapter 4 noise and Stage 2 emission limits, opening every major international airport to Volga-Dnepr's massive cargo fleet.

Airframe **RA-76503** represents the pinnacle of this modernization effort. Its construction originally commenced in Tashkent in 1997, but with the dissolution of Soviet supply chains, the airframe was painstakingly finished and completed its maiden flight on October 15, 2011.

## SQ Wings 1:200 Brand New Tooling Review

Until SQ Wings released this landmark model, diecast collectors who longed for a premium 1:200 scale Il-76 had virtually zero options—the type had only previously existed in 1:400 (AeroClassics) or 1:500 (Herpa).

SQ Wings invested heavily in developing a **brand-new all-metal 1:200 tooling**, and the result is nothing short of breathtaking:
* **Glazed Navigator Cockpit:** The signature multi-faceted glazed chin navigator cabin is rendered with precision-cast transparent inserts and ultra-thin painted framing.
* **PS-90A-76 Nacelles:** The massive modern turbofan cowlings correctly reflect the wide nacelle diameter and fan blade detail, distinctly setting this model apart from older narrow-cowl D-30 models.
* **Photo-Etched Antennas:** A complex array of communications blades, HF antenna masts, and pitot sensors are factory-installed on the dorsal fuselage and vertical stabilizer.
* **20-Wheel Undercarriage:** The complex 16-wheel main landing gear bogey system and steerable nose gear feature real rolling rubber tires and finely cast hydraulic shock-strut braces.
